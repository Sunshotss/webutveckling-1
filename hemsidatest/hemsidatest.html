I LOVE WEEZER... but only like the first two albums. The first album, the blue album, has a very special place in my heart because it was my introduction to weezer. The blue album is one of the most creative albums i have ever heard, it does its own thing with trying to appeal to a more nerdy audience and mixing in more "strange stuff" like how in the sweater song (a song about sweaters) cuomo adds a whole conversation in and out of the song, I LOVE THIS, its so fun and creative and it still panders to a rocky audience by being, well, a rock album.
